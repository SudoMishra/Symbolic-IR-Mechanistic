Research program / experimental log

Backend IDs are transport keys, not model interfaces.

A staged investigation of what changes when UUIDs, database keys, and personal data are replaced with compact, request-scoped symbols before they enter a language model.

behavioral representational causal systems
Reading guide. This page is the program-level abstract. Each experiment below opens a self-contained record with its question, design, results, limitations, and provenance. The machine-readable study manifest is the accounting source of truth.
01 / why

The convenient representation may be the wrong abstraction.

Modern pipelines routinely paste document UUIDs, account numbers, transaction IDs, and tool keys into prompts because those values already exist in the application. That choice quietly couples a probabilistic model to storage-layer syntax.

Problem statement

High-entropy identifiers cost tokens, can be difficult to reproduce exactly, expose internal values, and may interfere with binding an answer to the evidence that supports it.

Narrow thesis

Render canonical semantic objects into short, typed, request-scoped handles for the model, then validate and rehydrate them in trusted deterministic software.

What must be separated

Transport cost, output formatting, source binding, answer quality, reasoning budget, internal geometry, causal influence, data exposure, and authorization are different measurements.

What would falsify it

Stable parity or consistent UUID advantages across controlled, paired tasks; no exposure reduction from canonicalization; or a boundary that cannot reject malformed and cross-request references.

02 / tldr

What the evidence supports so far.

Transport

UUIDs impose a measurable tax.

In the controlled baseline, long identifiers increased prompt tokens and local-runtime latency. The size varies by tokenizer, source count, and model.

Attribution

Binding degrades more consistently than answers.

Scaled MuSiQue results favored compact numeric labels in every primary model/source-count cell, while answer F1 did not show the same uniform movement.

Heterogeneity

The result is not universal.

Some high-capacity models approach ceiling, and the frontier extension split sharply by model. Capability and decoding configuration matter.

Mechanism

Facts stay stable; identifier states differ.

Linear CKA found high similarity for unchanged fact-token geometry, while identifier representations were more model- and depth-dependent.

Privacy

Canonicalization removes literal output exposure.

Typed and untyped masked entity conditions eliminated raw-value output exposure in the evaluated suite, while typed handles generally improved resolution over untyped ones.

Boundary

Validation is necessary, not semantic authorization.

The system rejected malformed and cross-request payloads, but deliberately wrong registered handles passed structural validation. Business rules remain separate.

Claim discipline

The program supports an engineering pattern, not a law that every model always performs worse with UUIDs. Each experiment page records counter-results, parser failures, uncertainty, and scope limits beside the headline result.

04 / sequence

From observation to an operational boundary.

AObserve

Measure cost, formatting, answer quality, and binding in paired behavioral tasks.

BStress

Move from controlled single-hop data to held-out and scaled multi-hop retrieval.

CAblate

Separate aliasing, literal reproduction, reasoning budget, and long-output effects.

DLocalize

Inspect representation geometry, then intervene on source-ID assignments and activations.

EOperationalize

Generalize to typed entities and test a deterministic mapping-vault boundary.

05 / next

Open questions and planned work.

open

Broader frontier replication

Extend the now-complete four-model API cohort to additional providers, model versions, and constrained decoding regimes without pooling away model-specific behavior.

open

Long-context interaction

Separate identifier entropy from total context length, retrieval order, KV-cache pressure, and realistic production prompt structure.

open

Component-level localization

With all six Stage 7 models balanced at eight semantic examples and 384 interventions each, the next step is to separate attention-head and MLP contributions within each model’s transition layers.

open

Semantic authorization

Compose structural handle validation with ownership, freshness, policy, and domain constraints that can reject a valid-but-wrong registered handle.
